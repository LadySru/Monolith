Rules of Engagement

Read This Before You Join

This is not a server for passive observers. These are the expectations, stated plainly.

🤝

Real Life Comes First

Go on vacation, get busy, disappear for a few weeks — no problem. This isn't a clocking-in arrangement. But if you never engage at all, that's a different story.

🎯

Contribute Something Real

Drop a take, share a clip, ask a question, respond to someone. The bar isn't high — it just can't be zero. If you're here, be here.

🚫

No Dead Weight

We prune regularly to keep the community tight and worth being in. A small, active group is worth ten times more than a bloated server full of lurkers. You've been warned — in a good way.
